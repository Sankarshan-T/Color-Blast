Color Blast!

The easiest way to grab colors from the web.

What is it?

Ever see a color on a website and wish you could use it yourself? Color Blast! is a simple tool that lets you "steal" any color you see and save it for later. It's like a digital highlighter for your browser.

Why you'll love it

  • Point & Click: Just hover over any part of a website to find the exact color.
  • Build Palettes: Save your favorite colors into groups for your next project.
  • Get the Code: Instantly copy "HEX" codes to use in Canva, Word, or Photoshop.
